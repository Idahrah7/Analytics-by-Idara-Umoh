## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses telecommunications customer data to identify churn patterns, understand customer behaviour, and uncover opportunities to improve retention.

The analysis explores three key questions:

- Which customer segments have the highest churn rates?
- How do contract types, customer service interactions, and data usage relate to churn?
- What are the most common reasons customers leave, and where should retention efforts be focused?

## 🔍 Key Insights

**1. Contract Type and Customer Churn**

Month-to-month customers recorded the highest churn rate at **46.29%**, compared with **11.29%** for one-year contracts and **2.78%** for two-year contracts.

**2. Customer Service Interactions**

Churn increased significantly among customers with repeated service calls, reaching **87.54%** for customers who made three calls and nearly **100%** for those who made four or more.

**3. Customer Demographics**

Senior customers recorded a higher churn rate (**38.22%**) than non-senior customers (**24.30%**).

**4. Data Plan Subscriptions**

Customers subscribed to unlimited data plans had a churn rate of **32.11%**, compared with **16.10%** among customers without unlimited plans.

**5. Geographical Churn Patterns**

Churn rates varied across states, with **California recording 63.24%**, highlighting the importance of investigating regional differences and customer sample sizes.

## 🛠️ Data Analysis Process

### 1. Data Preparation — Excel

- Reviewed customer records and prepared the data for analysis.
- Organised customer information for churn analysis.

### 2. Data Analysis — PivotTables & Power Pivot

- Analysed churn across contract types, demographics, customer service interactions, and data usage.
- Used PivotTables and DAX measures to calculate churn metrics and compare customer segments.

### 3. Data Visualization — Excel

- Developed an interactive dashboard featuring KPI cards, PivotCharts, and a state-level churn heatmap.
- Incorporated slicers for interactive filtering.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Customer retention:** Introduce targeted retention offers for month-to-month customers.
- **Customer service improvement:** Prioritise customers with repeated service calls and investigate unresolved complaints.
- **Demographic engagement:** Develop tailored support strategies for senior customers.
- **Data plan optimisation:** Review unlimited data plan pricing, service quality, and customer satisfaction.
- **Regional retention:** Investigate high-churn states to identify location-specific issues.

## ⚠️ Data Limitations

- The analysis identifies relationships between customer characteristics and churn but does not establish causation.
- State-level churn rates should be interpreted alongside customer counts.
- The dataset does not provide a complete timeline of customer activity, limiting analysis of churn over time.
