# Pizza Sales Performance Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes pizza sales transactions from 2015 to evaluate revenue performance, customer purchasing patterns, product popularity, and order value distribution.

Using Microsoft Excel, I prepared and analyzed the sales data, developed PivotTables, and built an interactive dashboard to identify trends and support data-driven business decisions.

## Business Objectives

- Track overall sales performance and key business metrics.
- Identify peak sales periods across months, weekdays, and times of day.
- Evaluate pizza performance by category, size, and product.
- Analyze customer spending patterns and order value segments.
- Provide actionable recommendations to improve sales and operational planning.

## Tools and Techniques

**Microsoft Excel**
- Data cleaning and preparation
- Power Query
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas
- Interactive slicers and timelines

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Performance

The business generated **$817,860.05** from **21,350 orders** in 2015. July recorded the highest monthly revenue at **$72,557.90**, while October recorded the lowest at **$64,027.60**.

### 2. Peak Sales Periods

Fridays collectively generated **$136,073.90** throughout 2015, making Friday the highest-revenue weekday. Afternoon sales contributed approximately **48.9%** of annual revenue, making this the most important time period for revenue generation.

### 3. Product Performance

Classic pizzas generated the highest category revenue at **$220,053.10**. Large pizzas contributed approximately **45.9%** of total revenue.

The Classic Deluxe Pizza led by quantity sold, with **2,453 pizzas**, while the Thai Chicken Pizza generated the highest product revenue at **$43,434.25**.

### 4. Customer Spending Patterns

High-value orders (at least $50) represented approximately **24.6%** of total orders but generated **48.7%** of revenue.

This indicates that higher-spending transactions make a disproportionately large contribution to overall sales.

## Business Recommendations

1. **Optimize staffing and inventory:** Prepare for stronger demand on Fridays and during afternoon periods.
2. **Prioritize high-performing products:** Maintain availability of popular pizza categories, sizes, and products.
3. **Increase average order value:** Test bundles, complementary items, and targeted promotions designed to encourage larger purchases.
4. **Review underperforming products:** Investigate low-revenue pizzas using additional information on costs, margins, and customer preferences before changing the menu.
5. **Monitor monthly performance:** Investigate the causes of weaker sales months and evaluate targeted promotional opportunities.
